01 / THE QUESTION

Did Goupil create a market—
or enter one?

The historical record often casts the firm's arrival in New York as a decisive beginning. This paper tests that account against eleven annual commercial directories, examining the art-related trades, their locations, and their trajectory before and after February 1848.

The evidence shows an expanding trade before Goupil's arrival and no lasting movement toward the firm's Broadway address afterward. The videos make the paper's spatial and institutional evidence visible over time.

↳ Methodological scope: Location-based visualizations use geocoded directory entries and inherit the coverage limitations explained in the paper. The maps should be read with those qualifications in mind.
